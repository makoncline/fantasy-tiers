Install ESPN Reader

  1. Download espn-reader.zip from the app owner. Unzip it into a folder you will keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked. Select the folder with manifest.json.
  4. Pin Fantasy Tiers ESPN Reader in Chrome's Extensions menu.

Ready to draft

Open your ESPN draft. Click ESPN Reader → Open assistant. Keep both tabs open in the same Chrome profile.

Make your picks in ESPN. After restarting Chrome or updating the reader, click Open assistant again.

Update or remove

To update, replace the files in the same folder. Click Reload on the reader's card at chrome://extensions. To remove it, click Remove.
