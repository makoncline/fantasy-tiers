Your draft plan

12 teams · Full PPR · Pick 7 · 16 rounds

Build a strong team, one pick at a time. Take good value. Stay flexible. You do not need a perfect pick.

The plan

  1. Build around running backs and wide receivers. Get strength at both positions. Do not force a fixed order of picks.
  2. Use roster need to break close calls. Do not pass on a much better player just to fill an empty spot.
  3. Take one good quarterback and one good tight end. An elite player is worth considering at a fair price. Do not reach because others start taking that position.
  4. Use the bench for RBs and WRs with upside. Favor players who could earn a bigger role. Skip backup QBs and TEs.
  5. Save kicker and defense for the last two rounds.

When it is your turn

  • Check that the assistant shows the latest picks. Keep two good options ready.
  • Start with the top recommendation. Compare close alternatives and read any injury warning.
  • If several players look similar, favor the position you need or a player unlikely to last until your next turn.

Read the numbers

ADJ: help for this pick. VAL: underlying value for your league. Higher is better. ECR: expert rank; lower is better. ADP: when players usually go, not how good they are.

Small score gaps are not guarantees. Tiers group similar players; overall and position tiers are different.
